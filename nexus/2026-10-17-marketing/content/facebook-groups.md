**Body (same facts, reword per group):**

SARS lists these on its tax invoice page:

1. The words "Tax Invoice" or "Invoice" at the top
2. Your name, address and VAT number
3. Your client's name and address (and their VAT number if they are a VAT vendor)
4. A unique serial number and the date
5. A description of what you supplied, and the quantity
6. The amount, the VAT in rand, and the total

SARS asks for more detail on tax invoices over R5,000 (including your client's VAT number if they have one), and a tax invoice is generally due within 21 days of the sale. Check SARS's tax invoice page for the full list.
