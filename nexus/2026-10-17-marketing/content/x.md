## Day 3: thread, "What a South African tax invoice includes" (7 posts)

**1/7**
What does a South African tax invoice need on it? Here is the plain list, from what SARS lists on its tax invoice page. A thread.

**2/7**
1. The words "Tax Invoice", "VAT Invoice" or "Invoice".
2. Your name, address and VAT number as the supplier.

**3/7**
3. Your client's name and address, and their VAT number if they have one. SARS asks for more detail on tax invoices over R5,000, and a tax invoice is generally due within 21 days of the sale.

**4/7**
4. A unique serial number and the date of issue. Keep the numbers in order. Do not restart each month.

**5/7**
5. What you supplied and the quantity. "Services" is vague. "Logo design, 1 concept round" is better.

**6/7**
6. The value, the VAT charged (show it in rand) and the total. For second-hand goods, say so on the invoice.
