# Campaign brief: invoify-za launch (NEXUS-Micro, test run)

**Product.** LD Industries' free South African tax invoice generator (fork of Invoify, MIT). Browser-based: fill a form, pick one of 13 templates, download a PDF. Add a VAT number and the invoice is titled "Tax Invoice" with both parties' VAT numbers and 15% VAT shown in rand. No account, no database; saved data stays in the browser, encrypted at rest. Interface in 18 languages. Source of truth for claims: `README.md`, `i18n/locales/en.json` (landing copy), `app/[locale]/terms` and `privacy` pages.

**Status.** Not deployed yet. Domain will be a subdomain of the LD Industries site. Use `{SITE_URL}` as the link placeholder everywhere.

**Goal.** First 500 invoices generated in the 2 weeks after go-live, and the site indexed for "free tax invoice generator South Africa".

**Audience.** South African freelancers, sole proprietors and small businesses (VAT-registered and not), plus bookkeepers who invoice for clients. Pain: Word/Excel templates, paid invoicing apps for a few invoices a month, unsure what SARS needs on a tax invoice.

**Offer.** Free, no account, no sign-up (see facts.md for allowed wording). A soft path to LD Industries (Stack'n finance app later, custom software now). No price claims.

**Budget.** R0. Organic only. Paid is out of scope for this run (Rhea later).

**Channels.** LinkedIn (founder posts), Facebook small-business groups, Reddit (r/southafrica, r/PersonalFinanceZA, r/smallbusiness rules permitting), X, WhatsApp share text, one SEO blog article. No TikTok/Instagram video in this run.

**Constraints.**
- No claim the product can't back up. "SARS-ready" style wording must be checked against what SARS actually requires for a tax invoice; avoid implying SARS endorsement.
- POPIA: privacy claims must match facts.md fact 2 (invoice details go to the server only to make the PDF and are not stored in a database; saved invoices stay in the browser).
- Nothing is posted or sent. Drafts only. Voice: plain, direct SA English, no hype, no em dashes.

**Done when.** Strategy, funnel, messaging and compliance notes are written; a 2-week launch calendar exists; every channel has paste-ready drafts; Evidence Collector passes each phase and Reality Checker passes the final kit.
